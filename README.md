3. The supplied Google Drive folder is used as the current course-access destination.
4. The 10 chat review cards are clearly marked placeholders because no actual review text/screenshots were supplied. Replace them with verified student feedback before publishing.
5. Review the legal wording with your final business policy before accepting payments.
6. YouTube embeds use the video IDs from the supplied links.
