About ENJOY CREATOR ACADEMY.

A Tamil-friendly learning platform focused on practical AI and creator education.

OUR IDENTITY

Learn AI. Create. Grow.

ENJOY CREATOR ACADEMY (ECA) is a learning platform built around practical AI creation. Its current flagship program is the AI Video Creation Master Course, covering prompting, AI images, video creation, avatars, stories, ads, voiceovers, songs, Gamma AI and InShot.

The academy is designed to make modern AI workflows easier to understand for Tamil-speaking learners while keeping the learning path practical and creator-focused.
